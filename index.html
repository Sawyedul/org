01 / ABOUT ME

A life shaped by displacement, education & purpose.

I am a Rohingya young leader and writer from Rakhine State, Myanmar, now building my education and community work in Bangladesh. My journey has taught me that education is not simply a path to personal opportunity—it can become a bridge to dignity, participation, and a stronger future.

My work connects civic engagement, human rights, youth leadership, education, creative writing, and emerging skills in statistics and data science.
